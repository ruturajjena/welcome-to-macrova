🚀 Getting Started
Yes — Macrova is free to download and use. Core features including food logging, daily macro tracking, and the Daily Blueprint are available for free. Premium features such as AI meal plans, AI workout plans, and the AI Health Lab weekly summary require a paid subscription.
During onboarding, Macrova will ask for your age, height, weight, activity level, and goal (Lose Weight, Gain Muscle, or Maintain). From this, it calculates your personalized Daily Blueprint — your daily calorie and macro targets. You can update these anytime from Profile › Settings › Goals.
Macrova is currently available on Android via Google Play. An iOS version is actively in development and will be available on the App Store very soon. Stay tuned to our Instagram (@macrova_ai) for the launch announcement.
Macrova is fully localized in 7 languages: English, Hindi, Odia, French, Spanish, German, and Japanese. You can change your app language from Profile › Settings › Language. More languages are coming soon.
📸 Food Logging
Tap the Log Food tab and select the camera icon. Point your camera at your meal and take a photo (or upload one from your gallery). Macrova's AI will analyze the image, identify the food items, estimate portion sizes, and log the full macro breakdown — calories, protein, carbs, and fat — in seconds.
The AI scanner is highly accurate for common dishes and packaged foods. Accuracy improves with well-lit, clear photos and a single dish per shot. For mixed-plate meals, the AI estimates based on visible ingredients. You can always manually adjust quantities after the scan if needed. For precise tracking, using the search-by-name method alongside photo logging gives the best results.
Absolutely. Tap Log Food and use the search bar to find any food by name. Macrova has a large global food database covering everyday meals, packaged products, and restaurant dishes. Search results show calories and macros so you can log the right item quickly.
The Daily Blueprint is your personalized daily target for calories, protein, carbs, and fat — calculated from your goal and body metrics. It updates as you log meals throughout the day.

Health Balance shows the ratio of healthy meals vs junk food in your daily intake. A 100% Healthy score means all calories logged that day came from nutritious meals — no junk food logged.
✦ AI Plans & Insights
Go to the Plan tab and tap Generate Plan. Macrova's AI creates a full weekly meal plan tailored to your calorie target, macro goals, dietary preferences, and health objective. Each day includes breakfast, lunch, and dinner with full nutritional breakdowns. You can regenerate the plan at any time for fresh meal ideas.
Under the Plan tab, switch to the Workout tab. Macrova generates a personalized weekly gym split (e.g. Push-Pull-Legs) based on your fitness level, available equipment, weekly session count, and session duration. Each day lists specific exercises with sets, reps, and rest times.
The AI Health Lab (found under the Insights tab) analyzes your logged data over 7, 15, or 30 days. It shows your macro averages vs targets, eating pattern analysis (junk meals, skipped days, best performance day), and generates personalized AI recommendations based on your real data. It's powered by your actual logs — the more consistent you log, the more useful the insights.
AI plan generations have a daily limit to ensure quality and manage server costs. Your quota resets every 24 hours automatically. Premium subscribers get a higher daily limit. If you need more generations, upgrading to a premium plan is the best option.
👤 Account & Data
You can delete your account in two ways:

In-app: Go to Profile › Settings › Account › Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your data.

Via email: Send a request to macrovatracker@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email. We process all deletion requests within 7 business days.
Yes. Macrova uses Supabase as its backend with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled — meaning your data is only accessible by you. We do not sell personal data. Food photos used for AI analysis are processed securely and not stored as permanent profile data after the nutrition estimate is generated. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Go to Profile › Edit Profile to update your weight, height, activity level, or fitness goal. Your Daily Blueprint will automatically recalculate based on the new values.
